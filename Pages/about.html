About Bash's Barbershop

Bash's Barbershop is a locally owned business that was established in 2020 in East London, South Africa.

The business was founded to provide affordable grooming services and to allow Bash to share his talent.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide successful, professional and convenient grooming services that leave our customers satisfied and confident.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the best and preferred barbers in South Africa by providing outstanding work that leaves customers satisfied.

Our Target Audience

  • Men and women of all ages
  • University students
  • Teenagers
  • Children aged 3 and above
